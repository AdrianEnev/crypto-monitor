**Evidence needed to resolve the blockers**

- **B1 — partially resolvable:** obtain an exchange-origin baseline roster before the first eligibility/ranking boundary and a complete, versioned change log through June 30, including retired/renamed/relaunched contracts. It must identify contract instances and effective contract type, base classification, quote/margin assets, all status transitions, launch and settlement/delisting times. Require explicit coverage/gap evidence; a list of located announcements cannot establish the full candidate population. No checked source supplies that record.
- **B2 — partially resolvable:** obtain official initial `PRICE_FILTER.tickSize` values (including pre-window launches) plus **every** effective change for every relevant historical contract through the window. Need intraday UTC timestamps precise enough to order changes relative to sweep and stop construction, identity mapping, and evidence that no updates are missing. Isolated old/new notices cannot establish uninterrupted validity between them. Candle/trade-price precision or GCD cannot substitute. Without this chain, exact sweep, stop and downstream trade effects remain unquantifiable.
